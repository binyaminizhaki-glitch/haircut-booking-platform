CONTEXT

CUTNOW is not an open directory where customers browse random service providers.

CUTNOW is a managed mobile barbershop network.

The platform takes responsibility for the service experience.

The customer chooses a service, location and time, uploads a haircut inspiration image or selects a saved haircut, receives three relevant barber matches, books one barber, completes a simulated payment and tracks the barber’s arrival.

The barber receives the job request, reviews the haircut brief, accepts the job, updates the booking status, completes the service and records the haircut details for future bookings.

The platform manages:

1. Professional verification

2. Relevant matching

3. Availability

4. Pricing

5. Arrival estimates

6. Booking status

7. Payment

8. Cancellation support

9. Replacement support

10. Cleanliness standards

11. Reviews

12. Haircut documentation

13. Repeat bookings

14. Group bookings

15. Efficient routes for barbers

The main customer promise is:

A verified barber who matches the requested haircut arrives at the chosen location with the necessary equipment, completes the haircut, cleans the area and records the haircut details for the next booking.

The main barber promise is:

CUTNOW brings customers, fills available working hours, groups jobs geographically, manages bookings and payments and helps barbers work independently without renting a permanent chair.

The product should take conceptual inspiration from:

1. Glamsquad for premium professional service at home

2. Uber for clear availability, estimated arrival and live status

3. Blacklane for quiet operational confidence

4. SQUIRE for authentic barber culture

5. Aesop for restraint, materiality and editorial presentation

6. Linear for interface precision and subtle motion

These are conceptual references only.

Do not copy the layout, branding, typography or visual identity of any existing company.

Uber is an interaction and service status reference only.

Do not use Uber’s visual language or transportation application aesthetic.

The visual identity should draw more strongly from contemporary editorial photography, premium hospitality, independent grooming culture and modern consumer services.
